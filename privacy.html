Placeholder content. The final policy will be supplied by Fit4Life NZ before launch.

1. What we collect

When you contact the gym or book training we may hold your name, phone number, email address and any health information you choose to share with your coach.

2. How we use it

Your details are used to run your training, contact you about sessions and keep you safe in the gym. We do not sell your information to anyone.

3. Health information

Anything you tell a coach about injuries or health conditions is used only to keep your training safe and appropriate.

4. Your rights

Under the Privacy Act 2020 you can ask to see the personal information we hold about you and ask us to correct it.

5. Contact

For privacy questions, call the gym on 021 781 225 or send us an email.
