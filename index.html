A local gym, not a franchise

Fit4Life NZ is Mangōnui's strength gym. You get coached lifting in a place where the person next to you is a neighbour, not a stranger with headphones in.

Beginners are the point here, not a problem. Your first session covers the basics with a coach, and you progress at a pace that suits your body and your week.

  • Locally owned and run in Mangōnui
  • Qualified trainers coaching every group
  • All levels welcome, from first session to first competition
